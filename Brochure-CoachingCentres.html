The idea

What GyanSaar is

Every coaching centre lives or dies on its top faculty. When a star teacher leaves, results dip that year and admissions the next. Meanwhile, students who miss a class have nothing to return to — no notes, no transcript, no way to catch up before the next one.

GyanSaar addresses both, together. Your teacher taps one button in class, teaches as always, and taps stop at the end. What comes out is a transcript of what was said, a lesson record for the institute, and a fresh set of practice questions matched to that specific class. Nothing is published without the teacher's approval. Nothing is stored anywhere the teacher cannot delete.

Vision

Where we're headed

Classrooms cannot be replaced. They can be extended — with the teacher's consent, at the point of teaching, in a form students can return to.

By 2030, every entrance-exam student in India should have a searchable record of every lecture their teacher gave, and every good teacher should own a durable archive of their own work.
How it works

Four steps, one button

01
Teacher taps Start
Phone can go in the pocket. Class continues, uninterrupted. Recording survives screen lock and calls.
02
Class ends, tap Stop
The recording uploads in the background. Works over patchy WiFi — queued locally if the connection drops.
03
Three artefacts appear
A searchable transcript, an institute lesson record, and a first-draft practice set built from what was actually taught.
04
Teacher approves
Edit, approve, or discard. Approved material goes to students in one tap. Discards leave no trace.

Who benefits

What each of you gets

For the institute

  • Less dependence on any single star faculty — the teaching is preserved.
  • Retention improves when students who miss a class can catch up before the next one.
  • Documented teaching quality as evidence for parents, and for internal reviews.
  • Faculty admin time drops — assignment-setting is drafted from every class.
  • Consistency across batches — same material, same standard, same session.

For the teacher

  • One tap. No new workflow to learn. If they can use WhatsApp, they can use this.
  • Their teaching is preserved — their voice, their method, their examples.
  • Thirty minutes saved per lecture on drafting practice questions.
  • Full control: approve, edit, or discard — and discards leave no institutional trace.
  • The teacher owns their tape. Nothing is shared without their sign-off.

For the student

  • Never miss a class again — every lecture becomes searchable text.
  • Practice matched to today — questions from the exact class they attended.
  • Revise from source — notes, transcript, and practice from one session.
  • Same standard, every batch — no gap between "morning batch" and "evening batch".
  • Their voice is not the subject — only the teacher's teaching is captured.

What GyanSaar deliberately does not do

  • We do not grade students automatically — every mark passes through a teacher.
  • We do not surveil teachers — the institute owner does not see discards.
  • We do not use classroom recordings to train AI models.
  • We do not sell teacher, student, or institution data.
  • We do not replace the classroom — the teacher remains the source.
  • We do not store raw audio anywhere our staff or yours can read it.
