Your top faculty teach. GyanSaar captures each class with their consent — as a searchable transcript, a lesson record, and a first-draft practice set. One button. The teacher owns everything.

The idea

Coaching centres live and die on their top faculty. When a star leaves, results dip. When a student misses class, they have nothing to return to.

GyanSaar solves both.

Vision
By 2030, every entrance-exam student in India should have a searchable record of every lecture — and every good teacher should own a durable archive of their own work.

What each of you gets
For the institute

Less dependence, better retention.

  • Star faculty risk drops — the teaching is preserved, not just the teacher.
  • Missed classes stop hurting. Students catch up before the next one.
  • Documented teaching for parents, reviews, and NAAC — no extra work.
For the teacher

One tap. Total control.

  • No new workflow — if they use WhatsApp, they use this.
  • Thirty minutes saved per lecture on drafting practice sets.
  • The teacher owns their tape. Approve, edit, or discard — no trace.
For the student

Never miss a class again.

  • Every class is searchable — text they can go back to, any time.
  • Practice matched to today. Questions from the class they attended.
  • Same standard, every batch — morning or evening, same session.

What GyanSaar deliberately does not do

  • Grade students automatically
  • Show discards to the institute owner
  • Train AI models on your classroom
  • Sell teacher, student, or institution data
