GyanSaar turns each class your trainer teaches — one tap, one phone — into a searchable transcript, a first-draft assignment, marked student submissions, and a 24×7 doubt-clearing tutor for students, grounded in what was actually taught. The trainer owns and approves everything.

How it works

1
Start
Trainer taps record on their phone. No new hardware, no new workflow.
2
Stop
Class ends. Recording uploads automatically — even over patchy WiFi.
3
Draft
Within minutes: transcript, lesson record, and a first-draft assignment appear.
4
Approve
Trainer reviews, edits, approves or discards. Approved material reaches students in one tap.
