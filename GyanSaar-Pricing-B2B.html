About GyanSaar

GyanSaar is a classroom instrument for Indian coaching institutes and colleges. Trainers record each class with one tap; the system returns a transcript, a first-draft assignment, graded student submissions, and a 24×7 doubt-clearing bot on WhatsApp — all grounded in that specific class. GyanSaar partners with institutes to lift trainer productivity, improve student retention, and generate documented evidence of teaching, without replacing the classroom.

Platform Access

Every GyanSaar institutional licence includes the trainer app, admin portal, and the full student side: WhatsApp doubt bot, weekly parent digest, and LMS access tied to the institute. GyanSaar analyses what the trainer taught — not the trainer themselves — and produces class-level and institution-level artefacts that help the institute understand coverage without scoring individuals.

  • Trainer app — one-tap recording, inline edit, approval and discard with no institutional trace
  • Per-class artefacts — searchable transcript, formatted lesson record, first-draft assignment set
  • Student LMS — access to approved lessons, practice sets, and graded submissions
  • 24×7 WhatsApp doubt bot — grounded in the student’s own class, not a generic textbook
  • Weekly parent digest — summary of what was taught, delivered on WhatsApp
  • Admin portal — usage data, coverage dashboards, payment and roster management

Onboarding & Success

Every GyanSaar partnership includes a named Launch Partner who works with your Academic Head to onboard trainers, tune extraction prompts for your subjects, and keep usage healthy through the pilot and into steady state.

  • Trainer onboarding — 15 minutes per trainer, in person or over video; consent docs tailored to your institute (DPDP-aligned)
  • Launch and Land workshops — two short sessions at the start of pilot to lock in the first recordings and set per-batch expectations
  • Weekly check-ins — health of recordings, approval rates, student engagement, and bot quality
  • Direct trainer support — a WhatsApp line for your trainers during the first month; shared issue tracker thereafter
  • Impact review — monthly data read-out for the Academic Head covering institution-level coverage, retention proxies, and parent engagement
