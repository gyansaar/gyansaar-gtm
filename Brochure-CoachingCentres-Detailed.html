The idea

What GyanSaar is

Every coaching centre lives or dies on its top faculty. When a star teacher leaves, results dip that year and admissions the next. Meanwhile, students who miss a class have nothing to return to — no notes, no transcript, no way to catch up before the next one.

GyanSaar addresses both, together. Your teacher taps one button in class, teaches as always, and taps stop at the end. What comes out is a transcript of what was said, a lesson record for the institute, and a fresh set of practice questions matched to that specific class.

Nothing is published without the teacher's approval. Nothing is stored anywhere the teacher cannot delete. And nothing about the recording process interrupts the teaching itself.

Vision

Where we're headed

Classrooms cannot be replaced. They can be extended — with the teacher's consent, at the point of teaching, in a form students can return to. When the physical class ends, the teaching should not end with it.

By 2030, every entrance-exam student in India should have a searchable record of every lecture their teacher gave, and every good teacher should own a durable archive of their own work.
How it works

Four steps, one button

01
Start
Teacher taps record. Recording survives screen lock, backgrounding, and phone calls. Phone can go in the pocket; class continues.
02
Stop
Class ends, teacher taps stop. Upload happens in the background over WiFi or mobile data; queued locally if the connection drops.
03
Draft
Minutes later, three artefacts appear: a searchable transcript, a lesson record for the institute, and a first-draft practice set from that class.
04
Approve
Teacher edits, approves, or discards each artefact. Approved material reaches students in one tap. Discards leave no trace.

Who benefits

What each of you gets

For the institute

Less dependence, better retention.

  • Star faculty risk drops — the teaching is preserved, not just the teacher.
  • Missed classes stop hurting. Students catch up before the next one.
  • Documented teaching quality for parents, internal reviews, and NAAC.
  • Faculty admin time drops as practice sets draft themselves.
  • Consistency across batches — same material, same standard, same session.
For the teacher

One tap. Total control.

  • No new workflow to learn — if they use WhatsApp, they use this.
  • Their teaching is preserved — their voice, method, and examples.
  • Thirty minutes saved per lecture on drafting practice questions.
  • Full control: approve, edit, or discard, with no institutional trace.
  • Discards never count against them in any metric or review.
For the student

Never miss a class again.

  • Every class is searchable — text they can go back to any time.
  • Practice matched to today. Questions from the exact class they attended.
  • Revise from source — notes, transcript, practice from one session.
  • Same standard, every batch — morning or evening, same material.
  • Their voice is not the subject — only the teacher's teaching is captured.

What GyanSaar deliberately does not do

  • Grade students automatically — every mark passes through a teacher
  • Surveil teachers — the institute owner does not see discards
  • Use classroom recordings to train AI models
  • Sell teacher, student, or institution data — to anyone, ever
  • Replace the classroom — the teacher remains the source
  • Store raw audio anywhere our staff or yours can read it
About us

GyanSaar is built in Delhi by a small team of engineers and educators who spent time inside real coaching centres and colleges before writing a line of code. Our design is consent-first: teachers own their recordings, students are never the subject, and the raw audio is inaccessible to anyone but the automated system.

We do not take advertising, we do not resell data, and we are not part of any of the well-known online-tutoring platforms. Our first cohort of institutional partners is being onboarded across NCR this quarter.
