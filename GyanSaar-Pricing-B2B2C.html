About GyanSaar for individuals

Not every student learns inside an institute, and not every trainer teaches inside one. For visiting faculty, home tutors, and students whose institute doesn’t yet offer GyanSaar, the student-paid tier opens the same classroom instrument — recording, transcript, assignments, bot and digest — on a direct subscription. The trainer brings their class; the student brings the subscription; GyanSaar sits in the middle.

Platform Access

Two linked experiences on one platform. A subscribing student’s access is anchored to a trainer; the trainer records, approves and publishes; the student receives, revises and asks.

  • For the student — LMS login, 24×7 doubt-clearing bot grounded in their own class, weekly WhatsApp study digest, searchable archive of every class they attended, graded assignments ready for review
  • For the trainer — recording app, approval queue, LMS admin for their subscribed students, same artefact pipeline as the institutional product (transcript, assignment, grading)
  • Cross-sell to institutes — an institute already on a GyanSaar B2B plan can invite its students onto the student subscription as a home-use extension, with a modest share of the subscription back to the institute (see below)

Onboarding & Success

Zero friction for students; light touch for trainers. No sales call needed for a solo trainer to sign up and start recording.

  • Student — 7-day free trial, UPI auto-debit or card autopay, cancel anytime from the LMS
  • Trainer — self-serve sign-up, 10-minute onboarding video, WhatsApp support during the first month
  • Payouts — revenue-share payouts to trainers monthly via UPI, once accrued earnings cross ₹500

Trainer Revenue Share
20 % of each student subscription is paid back to the trainer, for every subscribed student past the first 5, for as long as both the student and the trainer stay active. A Trainer Pro with 25 students earns roughly ₹1,196 / month; at 35 students the share more than offsets the Pro fee.
